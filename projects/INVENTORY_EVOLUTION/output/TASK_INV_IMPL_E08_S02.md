# TASK_INV_IMPL_E08_S02 — Typed Dated Demand & Supply Projection

## Status
`DESIGN_READY_BLOCKED_BY_E07_GATE`

## Objective
Give MRP one normalized, dated view of demand and supply without letting it scan arbitrary business tables and guess intent.

## Demand classes
Initial supported sources:

- released WO component requirements;
- planned production demand;
- make-to-order firm sales demand where policy applies;
- project/prototype demand;
- safety-stock replenishment target;
- dependent demand generated by released MBOM explosion.

Each demand row exposes material, site, quantity, need date, type, source document, firm/planned state and priority.

## Supply classes
Initial supported sources:

- E07 accepted/nettable on-hand from E02 positions;
- approved open PO remaining quantity on expected date;
- released WO expected output;
- confirmed transfer inbound;
- confirmed subcontract return;
- explicitly firm planned supply where policy permits.

## Exclusions
Never count as normal supply by default:

- quarantine/rejected/rework/scrap;
- reservations owned by other demand;
- engineering/golden sample stock;
- unapproved substitute stock for a constrained requirement;
- draft/cancelled PO or WO;
- expired/restricted lot;
- stock owned by another party.

## Invariants
- one physical quantity cannot simultaneously satisfy two firm demands;
- remaining PO supply = ordered minus received/cancelled quantity, not original PO quantity;
- source cancellation/removal is represented explicitly and can create reschedule/excess exceptions;
- every projected row retains its authoritative source reference.

## Tests
Cover reservation exclusion, quality-state exclusion, partial PO receipts, cancelled/draft supply, transfer due date and subcontract scheduled-return date.

## Done
MRP receives one deterministic demand/supply interface with dates and provenance; no planning logic reaches around it to infer business meaning.

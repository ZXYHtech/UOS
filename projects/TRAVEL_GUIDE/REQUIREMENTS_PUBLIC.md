# TRAVEL_GUIDE Public Requirements — Frozen V1

## Scope

Create five evidence-based, genuinely distinct one-day Dujiangyan travel schemes for two adults traveling from Chengdu by suburban rail via Xipu. The trip should start early enough to preserve a full sightseeing day and return to Chengdu in the evening.

## Experience goals

- First-time experience of Dujiangyan local culture, history, food and everyday atmosphere.
- Comfortable pacing rather than maximum attraction count.
- Walking target: moderate / B level, approximately 15,000 steps for the day, with rest buffers.
- Include the paid Dujiangyan Scenic Area rather than designing a no-ticket route.
- Food plan must combine local snacks, a lighter/casual eating experience, and at least one more formal seated meal suitable for a relaxed conversation.
- Normal Sichuan spiciness is acceptable; one traveler prefers relatively little leafy-green-vegetable emphasis.
- Total trip spending target: <= CNY 500 for two people, excluding gifts already owned before the trip unless explicitly modeled as sunk cost.

## Research requirements

Research and cite current evidence for:

1. Dujiangyan geography and transportation access.
2. Dujiangyan irrigation-system history, culture and local customs.
3. Recent 2-3 year visitor reviews, including praise, complaints, queues, fatigue and common mistakes.
4. Representative foods, food districts, price bands and practical meal options.
5. Common and recommended one-day route patterns.
6. Recent news, temporary works, closures, events, crowd-control or service changes.
7. Visitor age structure and trip-purpose mix, only when traceable to a credible source; otherwise report the evidence gap or clearly labeled proxy.
8. Seasonal/monthly visitor-volume patterns, weather and normal-weekend crowd behavior, with no fabricated percentages.
9. Current ticket price, opening hours and optional paid transport inside the scenic area.
10. Current rail options and station-choice tradeoffs; do not assume that the originally named arrival station is optimal without comparison.

## Five scheme families

A. Balanced classic first-timer route.
B. History and culture depth route.
C. Food + Guanxian old-city atmosphere route.
D. Relaxed comfort / low-rush route.
E. Weather- and crowd-resilient fallback route.

The five schemes must differ materially in sequence, emphasis, pacing and tradeoffs rather than merely reordering the same stops.

## Deliverables

- Five route maps, each as SVG + PNG preview.
- Five complete phone-first guide PDFs; private timing/communication/gifting layers are stored encrypted in canonical UOS artifacts.
- One research-digest PDF + preview PNG.
- One pre-trip communication guide stored encrypted.
- One last-minute refresh close to departure for trains, weather, opening notices and crowd risks.

## Map accuracy requirement

Maps are operational artifacts, not decorative diagrams. Every named POI, station, entrance/exit, restaurant-area anchor and transfer point must be validated against current sources before publication. See `MAP_VALIDATION_POLICY.md`.

## Privacy boundary

This public file intentionally excludes exact home/origin, exact private travel date and timing, relationship context, relationship-specific communication strategy, gift details, photo/camera history and other private personal context. Those fields exist only in encrypted private context.

The full UOS project must remain at or below 50 canonical tasks.

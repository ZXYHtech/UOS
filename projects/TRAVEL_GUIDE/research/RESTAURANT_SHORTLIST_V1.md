# TRAVEL_GUIDE — Formal-meal shortlist V1

Refresh: 2026-09-02

Goal: one comfortable seated Sichuan/local meal for two people, normal spice, no need to emphasize leafy vegetables, convenient to the Dujiangyan Scenic Area / Guanxian Ancient City / Nanqiao cluster, and compatible with the <= CNY 500 total-day target.

## Tier 1 — best current fit

### 庭院小酌（瑞莲街店）
- Current platform signal: about CNY 60/person, 4.5/5, 35 reviews.
- Current listed hours: 11:00-21:00.
- Address: 瑞莲街31-35号（古县衙停车场幸福路出口处）.
- Fit: courtyard-style environment, seated Sichuan meal, good geographic fit for old-city / scenic-area transition, price compatible with two-person budget.
- Menu fit: meat/fish/rabbit/chicken dishes are available; leafy vegetables do not need to dominate the order.
- Planning use: current first-choice formal-meal candidate for Schemes A/D/E, subject to Saturday opening/queue refresh.

### 西街往事私房菜
- Current platform signal: about CNY 59/person, 4.5-4.6/5, 16 reviews.
- Current listed hours: 11:30-21:00.
- Address: 西街帅家巷5号 / 西街104号附近（platform address variants; final navigation pin must be refreshed before use）.
- Fit: quieter courtyard/private-kitchen character, inside/near the old-city walking zone, appropriate for an unhurried meal and conversation.
- Caveat: some sources describe set-style / arranged ordering and minimum-spend patterns; confirm current two-person ordering method before final selection.
- Planning use: strong alternative for Schemes C/D when a quieter meal matters more than river view.

## Tier 2 — location strong, budget/experience tradeoff

### 玉垒锦绣酒楼（南桥店）
- Current platform signal: about CNY 122/person, listed 09:00-24:00, location at 公园路1号 near the visitor center / Nanqiao.
- Fit: excellent sightseeing location and seated meal format.
- Drawback: two-person meal can consume ~CNY 240 before drinks/extras, which materially compresses the total CNY 500 day budget; current platform rating signal is also weaker than Tier 1.
- Planning use: optional atmosphere upgrade, not base-budget default.

## Discovery-only candidate

### 斗犀·川西民居私房菜
- Current Amap place record confirms a restaurant at 西街102号 in the Nanqiao community.
- Environment/location signal is promising, but current price/hours/recent-review evidence is not yet strong enough for canonical recommendation.
- Planning use: keep as backup discovery candidate only.

## Recommended two-person ordering structure

For Tier 1 restaurants, target about CNY 110-140 total:
- 1 substantial meat/fish/rabbit/chicken main dish;
- 1 second savory dish or tofu/egg/staple-oriented dish;
- 1 soup/staple if needed;
- water/tea depending on restaurant.

Avoid ordering many dishes simply to make the meal look formal. A calm environment + enough time is more important than total dish count.

## Final choice logic

- If the day is going smoothly and both want a comfortable conversation: prioritize 庭院小酌.
- If the old-city slow-walk is the emotional center and a quieter courtyard is preferred: compare 西街往事.
- If river view/location becomes more important than budget: consider 玉垒锦绣 only as an explicit upgrade.

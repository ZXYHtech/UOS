# Dujiangyan Visitor Profile & Trip-Purpose Research

## Core conclusion

A reliable, public, current source giving **exact Dujiangyan Scenic Area visitor age percentages and trip-purpose percentages** was not found in the current research pass. The project must therefore **not invent these figures**.

## What is publicly verifiable

### Dujiangyan uses visitor-profile / tourism-big-data systems

Public reporting on Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan's smart-tourism system confirms that the destination operates tourism big-data capabilities for visitor-flow monitoring and visitor analysis. Public technology case pages also describe age, origin, stay duration and preference analysis as available dimensions in deployed tourism data systems.

However, those public pages do not expose a current official table of Dujiangyan Scenic Area age shares suitable for quoting as destination statistics.

### Strong evidence for broad visitor diversity

Recent official/news evidence supports that Dujiangyan serves multiple visitor groups:

- heritage/cultural tourists attracted by the irrigation system and world-heritage identity;
- family / parent-child and study-tour visitors, especially during school holidays and festival programming;
- younger leisure / social-media / night-tour visitors through old-city, riverfront and newer immersive products;
- domestic short-haul weekend visitors from Chengdu and Sichuan;
- international visitors, with 2026 summer inbound traffic reported as growing strongly.

This supports qualitative segmentation, not exact percentages.

## Age data — evidence gap

A current public Dujiangyan-specific age distribution such as `18-25 = X%`, `26-35 = Y%` was not located from a credible government/statistical publication.

### What must NOT be used as a substitute

- Age data for a shopping mall in Dujiangyan.
- Generic Chengdu tourism age statistics.
- A small student survey / questionnaire without published sampling results.
- Platform audience demographics unless clearly labeled as platform-user data rather than destination visitors.

These can be used only as explicitly labeled proxies if needed for context, not as 'Dujiangyan visitor age ratio'.

## Trip-purpose mix — evidence gap

No current official source was found giving a clean percentage split such as:

- cultural sightseeing X%;
- leisure vacation Y%;
- food / social-media Z%;
- study tour W%.

The destination's program mix and news coverage clearly show these purposes exist, but exact proportions are not publicly established in this pass.

## Safe qualitative purpose categories for the final digest

1. **World-heritage / engineering / historical-cultural sightseeing** — core and directly tied to the site's identity.
2. **Weekend leisure / Chengdu-area short break** — supported by easy suburban rail access and destination positioning.
3. **Family / parent-child / study tourism** — visible in holiday and cultural-programming reports.
4. **Food / old-city / night atmosphere** — reinforced by Guanxian old-city and the 2026 night-tour expansion.
5. **Broader combined trips** — e.g. Qingcheng Mountain, Panda Valley or other Dujiangyan-area attractions, though these are not appropriate for this project's relaxed one-day core plan.

## Related verified data points

- In 2025, Dujiangyan's A-level scenic spots received 19.3935 million visitors; Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan received 10.0971 million according to the city's 2025 statistical bulletin.
- In 2024, Dujiangyan Water Conservancy Scenic Area reportedly received 7.8 million visitors, up 12.6% year on year.
- 2026 summer inbound tourism to Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan grew strongly; official-media reporting cited 21,900 overseas visitors from July 1-26, up 30.36% year on year.

These are volume figures, not age/purpose shares.

## Final-report wording requirement

The research digest should explicitly say:

> Publicly accessible current sources confirm large and diverse visitor flows, but this research did not find a credible official Dujiangyan-specific age-percentage table or trip-purpose-percentage table. Therefore, no exact percentages are asserted. Qualitative visitor segments are described separately from measured statistics.

# Dujiangyan Recent News / Operations — 2026 snapshot

## High-relevance current changes

### 1. New 2026 night-tour product: `都江·夜堰`

Red Star / Chengdu reporting says the immersive night-tour project was launched in June 2026, tested June 19-25, closed for optimization June 26-30, and entered normal paid operation from July 1. Adult ticket was reported at CNY 80.

**Planning impact:**

- Night-tour admission is a separate optional cost and should not be included by default under a tight two-person CNY 500 budget.
- The free Nanqiao / old-city riverfront atmosphere can still provide an evening ending without buying the separate night-tour ticket.
- Daytime visitors are subject to a latest-exit rule before night operations; final departure-day operating rules must be refreshed.

### 2. Daytime operating / latest-exit adjustments associated with night operations

Current major booking-platform notices state warm-season daytime check-in around 08:00-18:00 with last sales/check-in around 17:30, and a latest daytime visitor exit around 20:00 before night-tour operations.

**Planning impact:** the itinerary should not assume visitors can remain indefinitely inside the daytime scenic area after closing.

### 3. Strong capacity / crowd-control behavior remains active

2026 May Day official notices show Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan using active capacity-control and temporary entry pauses when thresholds are reached. Similar mechanisms have been used during other major holidays.

**Planning impact:** reserve the earliest practical entry window and maintain an old-city / food fallback block if crowd control delays entry.

## Current infrastructure / development watch

### Nanqiao-area cultural-block project

A 2026 tender notice exists for a `都江堰市南桥坊文化街区建设项目` design package. This establishes an active development project in the broader Nanqiao cultural-block context, but the current research does **not** establish that the bridge or the visitor walking route is presently closed.

**Rule:** do not convert a tender/design notice into a closure claim. The T-minus-1 check must confirm actual on-the-ground access before the final map/guide.

## No verified major closure found in this pass

This research pass did not find a credible current notice stating that the principal Dujiangyan Scenic Area hydraulic core, Nanqiao or Guanxian Ancient City is closed for the target early-September period.

This is **not** a guarantee of normal operation. Exact-date weather, maintenance, crowd-control and temporary notices still require refresh close to departure.

## News items useful for cultural context

### 2026 Dujiangyan Water-Releasing Festival / 放水节

Xinhua reported the 2026 water-releasing festival and its ceremonial re-enactment of historical maintenance / water-management traditions. This is useful cultural background even though the festival itself is not occurring on the travel date.

### Visitor growth / inbound tourism

2026 reporting shows continued strong domestic and international interest in the destination, supporting the assumption that even outside peak holidays the destination is a high-demand Chengdu-area attraction.

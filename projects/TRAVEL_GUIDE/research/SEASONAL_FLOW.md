# Dujiangyan Seasonal Visitor Flow & Crowd Research

## Core conclusion

Dujiangyan has strong year-round demand, with clearly documented spikes during major holidays and summer. Public evidence is sufficient to describe **relative seasonality and crowd risk**, but not to produce a trustworthy month-by-month percentage distribution for the scenic area. The project must not fabricate such percentages.

## Verified annual / large-period volume indicators

- 2024 Dujiangyan Water Conservancy Scenic Area: about **7.8 million visitors**, up 12.6% year on year.
- 2025 Dujiangyan A-level scenic areas: **19.3935 million visitors**; Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan: **10.0971 million** according to the 2025 statistical bulletin.
- 2026 Spring Festival: Dujiangyan A-level scenic areas received **1.0166 million visitors** across the holiday; Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan received about **620,000**.
- 2026 May Day: Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan was among Sichuan's leading 5A destinations and sold out on multiple days.
- 2026 summer: reporting indicates very heavy July traffic and strong inbound-tourism growth.

## High-risk crowd periods

### Spring Festival / major traditional holidays

Official Sichuan tourism reporting shows Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan issuing crowd warnings during Spring Festival 2026. Holiday cultural programming substantially increases traffic.

### May Day

One of the strongest crowd-risk windows. In both 2025 and 2026 the destination was reported as selling out or reaching very high loads. This should not be used as a proxy for a normal September weekend, but it proves the destination can hit capacity.

### Summer vacation

July-August combines family/study tourism, inbound tourism and Chengdu-area heat-escape demand. Current 2026 reporting repeatedly describes strong summer traffic. A recent People's Daily Sichuan report said overseas visitors to Qingchengshan-Dujiangyan from July 1-26 reached 21,900, up 30.36% year on year.

### National Day / Golden Week

Historical and recent notices show that the scenic area can issue capacity warnings / sold-out notices. This remains a high-risk period.

## Lower / medium crowd periods

Normal non-holiday weekdays are expected to be materially calmer than the major-holiday peaks. Normal weekends remain busier than weekdays, especially in good weather.

The current project targets a normal early-September Saturday, so planning should assume **moderate weekend traffic with localized queues**, not May-Day-level saturation. Exact-date conditions still require the T-minus-1 check.

## Time-of-day pattern for route design

Although exact hourly counts are not publicly exposed here, operational experience and crowd-control notices support these planning principles:

- **Early morning:** best chance to enter before the largest leisure-tour groups accumulate.
- **Late morning to early afternoon:** likely higher congestion at iconic hydraulic/bridge nodes.
- **Late afternoon:** central heritage traffic may ease somewhat as some day groups leave; old-city / food / riverfront activity begins to strengthen.
- **Evening:** Nanqiao / old-city riverfront can become busy for night atmosphere, especially on weekends and during night-tour operations.

These are planning inferences and must be labeled as such in the final research digest.

## Weather / season interaction

Early September in the Chengdu-Dujiangyan region can still feel warm and humid, with possible convective showers. Heat and rain can materially alter walking comfort, bridge/riverfront experience and the usefulness of long exposed sections.

Therefore the five-scheme architecture must include:

- a classic early-start scheme;
- a relaxed scheme with more shaded/rest blocks;
- a weather/crowd-resilient scheme that can move meal/rest/old-city blocks around rain or peak crowd windows.

## What the final report may and may not claim

### Safe claims

- Major holidays and summer are documented high-demand periods.
- Normal weekends are a meaningful but lower crowd risk than Golden Week / May Day.
- Early entry is a rational crowd-management strategy.
- Night riverfront demand can rise due to the 2026 night-tour product and free Nanqiao atmosphere.

### Unsafe claims

- 'September accounts for X% of annual visitors' — not supported by current evidence.
- 'Saturday is exactly Y% busier than weekday' — not supported here.
- Exact hourly percentages without an official dataset.

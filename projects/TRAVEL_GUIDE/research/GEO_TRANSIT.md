# Dujiangyan Geography & Transit Baseline — 2026-09 research snapshot

## Core conclusion

For a first-time one-day visit focused on the Dujiangyan Scenic Area + Guanxian old-city / Nanqiao cluster, **Lidui Park railway station (离堆公园站) is the default arrival station to compare against**, not Dujiangyan railway station. The final itinerary must still check exact departure-day trains and seat availability before booking.

## Why station choice matters

### Lidui Park station

- Current rail-search pages show direct Xipu -> Lidui Park suburban trains in roughly 23-30 minutes, commonly CNY 10 one way.
- Current traveler/traffic references consistently place Lidui Park station closest to the scenic-area / old-city cluster.
- The station is associated with the Yangtianwo / old-city side of central Dujiangyan, making it practical for a walking-first day.
- It reduces the need for a second taxi/bus transfer before the first attraction.

### Dujiangyan station

- Current Xipu -> Dujiangyan services are also frequent and commonly CNY 10.
- Dujiangyan station is useful as a **return-station resilience option** because current evening timetable snapshots show multiple trains toward Xipu around 20:00-21:30.
- It is not automatically the best first-arrival point for a scenic-area / Nanqiao / Guanxian-old-city-centered day because an onward local transfer is usually required.

### Evening return resilience

This makes a mixed strategy worth testing: arrive Lidui Park for convenience, then choose Lidui Park or Dujiangyan for return depending on the final evening plan and actual ticket availability.

## Ticket and opening baseline

Chengdu municipal-government destination information in 2026 lists the Dujiangyan Scenic Area ticket at **CNY 80 per adult**. Current major travel platforms list daytime opening around **08:00-18:00** in the warm season, with last ticket/check-in around 17:30. Final planning must refresh the official/current operating notice immediately before departure.

## Planning implications

- Early arrival is worthwhile because it gives flexibility to tour the hydraulic system before peak heat/crowds and reserves late afternoon/evening for old-city food and atmosphere.
- Do not overfill the day with remote attractions such as Qingcheng Mountain or Panda Valley; they conflict with the one-day first-timer cultural/food/comfortable-pacing goal.
- Build at least 30-45 minutes of total schedule buffer for rail interchange, station exit, ticketing, rest and navigation errors.
- The final route should treat 15,000 steps as a planning target, not a quota.

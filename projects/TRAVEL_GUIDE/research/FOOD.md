# Dujiangyan Food Research — one-day trip baseline

## Objective

Support a two-person one-day plan that combines:

1. several local snacks / street-food tastes;
2. one lighter or casual meal/rest experience;
3. at least one more formal seated meal suitable for an unhurried conversation;
4. a total-trip budget that remains practical after rail and admission costs.

## Representative local foods with repeat evidence

Across current local-tourism food pages and major travel-platform listings, the following appear repeatedly as representative Dujiangyan / old-Guanxian foods:

- 葱葱卷 — thin wrapper + shredded vegetables / sauce; distinctive local snack.
- 赵卖面 / 甜水面 / 开口小饺子 — old-city noodle/dumpling snack category.
- 尤兔头 — spicy or five-spice rabbit head; iconic but messier and more niche.
- 钟鸭子 / 甜皮鸭类卤味 — portable/shareable meat snack.
- 醪糟 / 红糖醪糟粉子 — good as a sweet rest stop.
- 冰粉 — useful warm-weather light dessert/rest stop.
- 豆花饭 — inexpensive local-style casual meal.
- 蹄花 / 家常烧菜 / 川西江湖菜 — stronger candidate categories for the formal seated meal.

## Food-zone logic

The most efficient food research should focus on **Guanxian Ancient City / Yangliu River Street / Xijie / Nanqiao-adjacent old-city streets** because these areas fit naturally after or around the scenic-area visit. This avoids spending the day crossing the city solely for a restaurant.

Current Dujiangyan tourism-food pages identify old-city streets and names such as Zhao Maimian, Zhong Yazi, You Tutou and congcongjuan as representative local choices.

## Formal-meal design criteria

A restaurant used as the planned formal meal must pass all of these before the final PDF:

- currently operating at the relevant meal time;
- reasonably close to the chosen itinerary rather than requiring a long detour;
- seated indoor or shaded environment;
- not excessively noisy if another nearby option is available;
- two-person order can be built around roughly CNY 100-160 total if possible;
- menu can avoid making leafy vegetables a central part of the meal;
- normal Sichuan spice level available;
- at least two non-snack dishes so the meal feels intentional rather than a rushed refill stop;
- current map/business evidence and preferably current user-review evidence.

## Budget logic

Known fixed baseline before local transport/food:

- Dujiangyan Scenic Area: about CNY 80 x 2 = CNY 160.
- Suburban rail: about CNY 10 x 2 travelers x 2 directions = roughly CNY 40.

This leaves about **CNY 300** under a CNY 500 total target before local vehicle costs and food.

Recommended food budget envelope for scheme design:

- snacks / dessert total: CNY 40-70 for two;
- formal meal: CNY 100-160 for two;
- drinks / water: CNY 20-40;
- leave CNY 40-80 for local transport / unexpected costs rather than spending the full remainder on food.

## Practical sequencing

Avoid eating every famous item. A better one-day structure is:

- one small savory local snack;
- one sweet/cooling rest item;
- one proper seated meal;
- optional second snack only if appetite and time allow.

This produces more comfort and leaves more uninterrupted time for the heritage visit and old-city atmosphere.
